The Fighter · Stage 1

Chapter 1 — Fresh Spawn

In Lilyhollow, a frog earns their flies one way: bounties. The Guild board by the boardwalk is never empty, and the rain never quite stops.

Tad signed his name in the Guild book tonight. Every hunter picks a class: Lashers with their whip-tongues, Dartcallers with poison blowpipes, Shellwardens behind shields as heavy as a cart. Tad picked the one the others laugh at, the Bogbrawler, who fights with nothing but his fists.

His first poster pays three flies. It is a start.
